### Why no JR Pass or Hakone Freepass

A [seven-day Ordinary JR Pass](https://japanrailpass.net/en/purchase/price/) costs **¥50,000/adult online** (current agency price ¥53,000). The preferred return Nozomi also needs a [¥4,960 pass-holder special ticket](https://japanrailpass.net/en/use/special-ticket/): **¥54,960/adult**, versus ¥26,970 for these individual Shinkansen tickets. Even Hikari both ways is only ¥26,650/adult. A seven-day pass cannot cover both airport dates across Dec 8–16, and [private Kintetsu/Odakyu/Osaka Metro/Hakone services are outside its coverage](https://japanrailpass.net/en/about_jrp/route/).

The [Hakone Freepass](https://odakyu-global.com/passes/hakone-freepass/) is also unnecessary for the present plan. Dec 10–12 needs a **three-calendar-day** product: area-only ¥6,400/adult; Shinjuku version ¥7,500, with Romancecar surcharge extra. Even two Odawara–Gora rail legs plus a short museum-train return are about ¥1,860/adult, and free shuttles reduce that further. Recalculate only if choosing a ropeway/lake/bus loop instead of the relaxed Gora plan.

1. **Now:** prepare [SmartEX](https://smart-ex.jp/en/) account/payment access, measure suitcases, and confirm the Karaku shuttle and meal/checkout details. Do not store account/payment details in this repo or the public companion.
2. **Romancecar:** book through [official English EMot](https://www.emot-tickets.jp/express-menu?language=en). All seats reserved; its limited-express ticket is **in addition to the basic fare**, which can be paid by Suica/PASMO. [Operator guide and release rule](https://odakyu-global.com/passes/romancecar/).
3. **Shinkansen:** use the monthly releases above to compare actual trains and choose two adjacent seats. SmartEX also accepts limited advance requests from one year ahead, with payment then; train/time/seat details remain tentative until the monthly schedule is finalized. Early requests do not guarantee exact D/E or baggage seats. [Official advance-booking rules](https://smart-ex.jp/reservation/useful/pre_time/).
4. Prefer **D + E** on the two-seat side; **E is the Fuji-side window in either direction**. Weather may hide Fuji. Prioritize adjacent seats and required luggage space. [JR Central seat advice](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/goldenroute/shinkansen/).
5. Choose basic flexible tickets unless a specific discount's restrictions suit the final plan. [SmartEX basic](https://smart-ex.jp/en/product/plan/service/) permits changes before ticket pickup/gate entry and the booked departure, paying fare differences; its predeparture refund fee is ¥320/person. Discount rules differ.

### Bags and local IC cards

Measure **height + width + depth, including wheels/handles**. A suitcase **over 160 cm and up to 250 cm** needs a seat with oversized baggage area, at the normal reserved fare. Reserve at the monthly window; the rear space is shared. Over 250 cm is not allowed. Do not rely on paying an onboard fee to solve unavailable baggage space. [Official baggage rules](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/info/oversized-baggage/).

Each traveler needs their **own** Suica/ICOCA/card/device. Use it for ordinary local trains and accepted buses, including regular Kintetsu to Nara. It does not by itself buy a Romancecar surcharge, N'EX ticket or Shinkansen reservation. Hakone Tozan railway accepts IC cards; the cable car requires a separate ticket/pass. If forwarding is confirmed, carry essentials for Hakone plus a delay buffer; neither shipment is arranged yet.

## Airport and Nara details

- **N'EX:** the [¥5,200 round trip](https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/multi/pass/nex.html) is valid 14 days and fits Dec 8/16, for eligible non-Japanese-passport holders. Ordinary reserved seats included; arrange the actual trains through [JR East reservations](https://www.eki-net.com/en/jreast-train-reservation/Top/Index) or the airport. Allow about 15–25 minutes from Shinjuku's N'EX platform to Hyatt, beyond the hotel's nine-minute West Exit walking description; its free shuttle is first-come and construction affects the stop. [Hotel access](https://hyattregencytokyo.com/en/access/).
- **Airport bus alternative:** the [official Narita–Shinjuku route](https://www.limousinebus.co.jp/en/timetable/detail/Narita-Shinjuku/?dir=1) lists Hyatt and **¥3,600/adult each way**. Choose an actual Hyatt-stopping service; not every Shinjuku bus stops there. Hotel guidance is about 120 minutes, with extra road-traffic margin needed.
- **Nara:** follow **Kintetsu Osaka-Namba** signs, not Nankai or the Metro. Regular Rapid Express/Express takes about 40–45 minutes station to station, **¥680/adult each way**, no reservation. Check the destination is Kintetsu-Nara. [Official train/ticket guidance](https://www.kintetsu.co.jp/foreign/english/about/howto/before.html) · [official fare breakdown](https://www.kintetsu.co.jp/foreign/english/aoniyoshi/). AONIYOSHI/other Limited Express is optional and costs extra; none booked. Use [Nara Kotsu's English map](https://www.narakotsu.co.jp/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/routemap-naraCity202604_EN.pdf) or a taxi for the park if desired; taxi fare is not quoted.
