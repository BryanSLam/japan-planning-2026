### Recommendation and tradeoffs

**Noazami (202): best value for this traveler.**

- 36.5 m² interior (about 393 sq ft), bedroom and adjoining eight-mat tatami sitting area.
- Two semi-double beds; official description emphasizes Mount Myojindake/Myojodake scenery (the Hakone mountain range, not Mount Fuji).
- Private open-air onsen; same shared-property facilities, with no extra privileges established for the higher room categories.
- Saves ¥27,200 / approximately $173 compared with Asebi on the observed plans.
- Tradeoff: less interior space and meals are outside the guest room, though in a private dining room.
- [Room details/photos](https://madokanomori.gorahanaougi.com/en/rooms/noazami/)

**Asebi (306): best upgrade if in-room dining and room time matter.**

- 59 m² interior (about 635 sq ft): approximately 62% larger than Noazami.
- Bedroom, eight-mat tatami room, and additional dining room.
- Shoji screens, decorative parquetry woodwork, HIDA Sangyo furnishings, Simmons beds with cedar frames.
- Two beds, each 125 × 200 cm; not an established bedding upgrade over Noazami's two semi-double beds.
- 13 m² bath deck versus Noazami's 12 m²: only a small difference. No verified larger tub or superior view.
- No extra bar, shared-bath, pickup, or complimentary-spa privileges were found.
- Costs approximately $46 more than Azumaichige, making it the stronger interior-space/in-room-dining upgrade if those matter.
- [Room details/photos/layout](https://madokanomori.gorahanaougi.com/en/rooms/asebi/)

**Azumaichige (211): choose mainly for the large outdoor deck.**

- 53 m² interior, 20 m² bath deck with privacy louvers/screens.
- Two single beds in the official overview.
- Bigger deck than both alternatives; do not infer a superior view from this.
- At the observed rates, Asebi adds more indoor space and in-room dining for ¥7,200 more; Noazami saves ¥20,000.
- [Room details/photos](https://madokanomori.gorahanaougi.com/en/rooms/azumaichige/)

### Amenities and operational checks

- Twenty guest rooms, all with private open-air hot-spring baths.
- Shared men's/women's bathing facilities, each with indoor and outdoor baths; earlier official research listed 15:00–24:00 and 05:00–10:00, to be rechecked.
- Bar Kodama: official page lists **20:00–00:00, last order 23:30, irregular closing days**. **Confirm December 10 opening before choosing the property because of its bar.** Drinks are not assumed included.
- Spa treatments and hot-stone bathing are extra-cost options, not assumed included in a room upgrade.
- Local pickup from Gora/Sounzan was described in official research. Exact pickup point, advance arrangement, and operating times need confirmation. Website/plan pages had differing start times (14:30 vs 15:00); do not promise an early pickup.
- Check-in 15:00–18:00; check-out 11:00 on the official room pages.
- Dinner slots listed 18:00/18:30/19:00; breakfast 08:00/08:30/09:00. Confirm actual availability when booking.
- Dietary restrictions matter: the checked plan says it cannot accommodate several restrictions, including fully vegetarian, complete seafood allergies, gluten-free, halal, or kosher requirements (pesco-vegetarian exception noted). Ask the traveler about needs before recommending payment; do not assume substitutions.
